Your language first. Streams for Stremio.

Pick your language: dubbed and dual-audio releases in it come first in each quality, with the original audio as a backup. Set it up here and it installs straight into your Stremio account.

Your other addons stay as they are. Dublix only adds or updates its own entries, and you get a backup before anything changes. Step-by-step guide

1Connect Stremio

  1. On a computer, open web.stremio.com and sign in with the account you use on your TV.
  2. Open the browser console (F12, then Console).
  3. Paste this line, press Enter and copy the text it prints: JSON.parse(localStorage.getItem("profile")).auth.key

The AuthKey lets this page read and update your addon list. It goes straight to Stremio and is never stored. Signing out of Stremio invalidates it.

2Debrid service optional

Strongly recommended

Without debrid, every play is a gamble.

Longer waits. More buffering. Fewer working streams. Keep a debrid service for a faster, more reliable experience.

Remove the service with × to continue without debrid. What is a debrid service?

Without debrid, playback depends on torrent seeds and may be slow, buffer or fail. Use a Stremio player with P2P support. Your IP is visible to peers.

3TMDB key optional

Strongly recommended. With it, Dublix checks titles and years, so you never get the next movie of a franchise by mistake. Get a free key in about two minutes.

Without a key, title and year checks are off: movies from the same franchise can be mixed up.

    1. Copy your Stremio AuthKey from web.stremio.com, signed in with the account you use on your TV.

    FAQ

    What does Dublix do?

    It builds an AIOStreams profile for the language you pick: it searches several sources, uses your debrid service or P2P torrents, puts releases in your language (dubbed or dual-audio) first in each quality and keeps one original-audio option, unless you choose to see only your language. Then it adds that profile to your Stremio account.

    How well does my language work?

    Only Portuguese (Brazil) was measured on real titles. The other curated languages (English, Spanish for Latin America and Spain, French, Italian, German) use the same method with their own keywords and sources, but they have not been measured on real streams. Languages marked basic rely only on AIOStreams' language detection: no keyword list and no extra sources, so labels and ordering are less reliable.

    Does my traffic go through someone's server?

    Video plays from your debrid service straight to your device, or from torrent peers when using P2P without debrid. The search runs on the AIOStreams instance you picked (a public one, or your own). This site only passes your configuration to that instance once, to create the profile; it stores nothing.

    What is a debrid service?

    A paid service (TorBox, Real-Debrid, AllDebrid, Premiumize, Debrid-Link, Offcloud) that fetches files on its servers and streams them to you over HTTPS. Easynews is a Usenet provider and works too.

    Can I use Dublix without debrid?

    Yes: remove the last service with × and accept the warning. Dublix will return P2P torrents and prioritize those with more seeds. Playback may be slower or unavailable and requires a Stremio player with P2P support. Browsers and some TV players need an external Stremio streaming server; the AIOStreams app does not play P2P torrents. Your IP is visible to torrent peers.

    Why do I need a TMDB key?

    It is optional, but it lets Dublix compare the title and year of each release with what you asked for. Without it, Harry Potter Part 1 can show Part 2 at the top. The key is free and stays in your own profile.

    Why can a label like Dual áudio or PT-BR be wrong?

    On a public instance the label comes from the file name, and some names lie. In our tests with Portuguese (Brazil), about one in six files named "dubbed" had no Portuguese track. Checking the real audio tracks needs a server; it is coming to the self-host kit.

    What do I lose without a server?

    About 95% of the list is the same (measured with Portuguese (Brazil)). A self-hosted instance also fixes Brazilian season-pack names, and partly Spanish ones (without it, some series can show the wrong episode first), and keeps your profile on your own machine. Audio-track verification is coming to the kit.

    How do I undo it?

    Use Restore a backup with the file saved before the install, or remove the Dublix addons in Stremio. Your other addons were never changed.

    Where are my keys?

    In your AIOStreams profile on the instance you chose, encrypted with the password this page generated. Save the profile details file to edit the profile later.
